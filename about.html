I invent worlds, then I make them playable.

I'm Ian Aviñó, a digital artist focused on 3D modeling and game development. I live in Catalonia and I graduated in Digital Arts from La Salle in Barcelona in 2026.

I build my own models and turn them into playable demos and small games. I model and texture in Blender and Photoshop, then bring everything to life in Unity, Unreal Engine 5 or GDevelop, programming the mechanics myself.

I love inventing fictional worlds and bringing them to life, from the first sketch to something you can explore.

I'm looking for a role as a 3D modeler and as a game developer, ideally somewhere I can do both.
